# Staff Backend Engineer — Acme (Real-Time Payments)

**About the role**
Acme is building a real-time payments platform. We're hiring a Staff Backend Engineer to lead the
design of high-throughput, low-latency services that move money reliably at scale.

**What you'll do**
- Architect and build Go microservices handling millions of transactions per day.
- Own reliability: drive down latency and error rates, and lead incident response and on-call.
- Design event-driven pipelines (Kafka) for settlement and reconciliation.
- Mentor engineers and raise the bar on testing, CI/CD, and observability.

**What we're looking for**
- 8+ years backend experience, with time at the Staff/Lead level.
- Strong Go (Python a plus); deep experience with distributed systems at scale.
- Hands-on with AWS, Kubernetes, and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Experience with Kafka or similar streaming platforms.
- A track record of mentoring and improving engineering practices.
- Payments, fintech, or other high-reliability domain experience preferred.
